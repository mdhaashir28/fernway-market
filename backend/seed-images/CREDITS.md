## Products that still need a photo

No freely licensed photo that clearly matches these products was found, so the apps show a clearly labelled
**“Photo coming soon”** placeholder for them (no made-up packaging). The staff dashboard marks them **No photo**; tick
**Missing photo only** on the Products page to list them.

- **Store-packed staples:** Toor Dal (`RD-001`), Chana Dal (`RD-003`), Ponni Raw Rice (`RD-008`), Idli Rice (`RD-009`), Dosa Rice (`RD-010`).
- **Hindustan Unilever brands:** Surf Excel, Rin, Wheel, Vim, Domex, Comfort (`HH-005` – `HH-013`); Lux, Lifebuoy, Dove, Pears, Hamam,
  Clinic Plus, Sunsilk, Close Up, Pepsodent, Vaseline (`PC-001` – `PC-011`).
- **Nestlé brands:** Maggi (`PA-007`, `PA-008`), Milkmaid (`PA-009`), Nescafé (`DR-006`, `DR-007`), Nestlé a+ and Everyday
  (`DE-006` – `DE-008`), KitKat and Munch (`SN-005`, `SN-006`).

Branded packaging is protected by copyright and trademarks, so use photos you are allowed to use: photograph the
packs on your own shelves, or use images your distributor or the brand gives you permission to use.

**How to add a photo:** Staff dashboard → Products → open the product → **Upload photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB,
square looks best). Or, for the sample data, save the file as `products/<SKU>.jpg` here (for example
`products/RD-001.jpg`), add a row to the table above, and run `npm run db:seed` — it fills in sample photos for
products that don't have one yet and never replaces a photo you uploaded.
